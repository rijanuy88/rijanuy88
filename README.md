## How I approach systems

I prefer systems that are **well-scoped, testable, documented, observable, and maintainable**. Automation should reduce operational friction without creating hidden technical debt or fragile dependencies.

1. Understand the business process and success criteria.
2. Map systems, data, handoffs, and failure points.
3. Choose the simplest architecture that can scale responsibly.
4. Build with clear separation of concerns and reliable integrations.
5. Test edge cases, errors, permissions, and operational handoffs.
6. Document the system so another person can understand and operate it.
7. Improve based on usage, feedback, and measurable outcomes.
